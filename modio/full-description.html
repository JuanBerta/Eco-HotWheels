Hot Wheels Rework is an add-on to the Hot Wheels mod by Zangdar, for the latest stable Eco release (0.14.2.1). Hot Wheels is a required dependency: mod.io installs it automatically, and it provides all the vehicles and chargers. This add-on adds a new farming vehicle and solar mode for the Wall Charger on top.

Multi-Row Wheel Hoe

  • A pushable wheel hoe with one front wheel, two long handles and three rows of tines, with its own 3D model.
  • Grab the handles (E) to push it. As it moves, it tills a strip three blocks wide in front of it.
  • Click it while on foot to switch plowing off or back on.
  • Plowing costs no calories.
  • Crafted at the Wainwright Table with Basic Engineering level 3, from iron bars, hewn logs, wood boards, a wooden wheel and lubricant.

Solar mode for the Wall Charger

  • The Wall Charger gets a Solar Charging tab with its status and a Toggle Solar Mode button.
  • With solar mode on, the charger uses no grid power and charges the connected vehicle only in daylight (06:00 to 18:00 on the sky clock).
  • At night it switches itself off and the vehicle stays connected. It switches back on in the morning.

Installation

  1. Subscribe on mod.io. Hot Wheels by Zangdar is installed with it.
  2. For a manual install, first install Hot Wheels as its page describes, then extract this zip into Eco_Data/Server/. It contains Mods/UserCode/HotWheelsRework/ with the source files, the Hoe bundle and modinfo.json. Remove any older HotWheelsRework.dll from your Mods folder.
  3. Restart the server.
