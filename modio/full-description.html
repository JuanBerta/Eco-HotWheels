Hot Wheels brings new ground, water and electric vehicles to Eco, ported to the latest stable Eco release (0.14.2.1). None of the vehicles need food to drive.

Vehicles

  • Scooter
  • Bicycle
  • Moto
  • School Bus
  • Steam Paddle Craft
  • Tesla Model 3 (electric)
  • Jet Ski

Electric charging

  • Wall Charger: charges electric vehicles at 2,350 W from the grid. Use its Charger tab to connect or disconnect a vehicle, or to toggle Solar Mode.
  • Solar Mode: charges vehicles from daylight, with no grid power used. At night the car stays connected and charging pauses until morning.
  • Tesla Supercharger
  • Electric vehicles show a trip odometer and battery used per trip in their status.

Installation

  1. Download the zip (contains HotWheels.dll, HotWheels-v3.2.0.unity3d and modinfo.json).
  2. Extract it into Eco_Data/Server/Mods/HotWheels/ on your Eco server.
  3. Restart the server.
